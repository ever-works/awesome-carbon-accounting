## Carbon Accounting Platforms

- [Brightest](https://brightest.io/) - Carbon accounting and ESG software for SMEs and enterprises to measure emissions, set targets, and generate sustainability reports. Offers a free plan for small businesses and paid tiers. Freemium SaaS. ([Read more](/details/brightest.md)) `Freemium` `Paid` `SME` `ESG`
- [Greenly](https://greenly.earth/) - Carbon accounting platform for SMEs and mid-market companies to measure emissions, reduce carbon footprint, and comply with regulations. Offers automated data collection and reduction recommendations. Freemium and paid plans. ([Read more](/details/greenly.md)) `Freemium` `Paid` `SME` `API`
- [Microsoft Sustainability Manager](https://dynamics.microsoft.com/sustainability-manager) - Cloud-based sustainability management solution within Microsoft Cloud for Sustainability. Tracks emissions, waste, and water usage across operations and value chains. Paid Dynamics 365 licensing. ([Read more](/details/microsoft-sustainability-manager.md)) `Paid` `Enterprise` `Cloud` `Microsoft`
- [Normative](https://normative.io/) - Carbon accounting software for businesses to measure emissions across scopes 1, 2, and 3 using activity and spend-based data. Provides science-based targets support and ESG reporting. Paid subscription with tiered plans. ([Read more](/details/normative.md)) `Paid` `API` `Scope 3` `SME` `Enterprise`
- [nZero](https://nzero.com/) - Carbon management platform that provides 24/7 granular emissions tracking for utilities, governments, and enterprises. Uses interval data for precise carbon accounting. Paid enterprise subscription. ([Read more](/details/nzero.md)) `Enterprise` `Paid` `Utilities` `Real-Time`
- [Persefoni](https://persefoni.com/) - AI-powered carbon accounting and ESG reporting platform for enterprises and financial institutions. Supports GHG Protocol compliance, SEC climate disclosure, and CSRD reporting with audit-ready data. Paid enterprise software. ([Read more](/details/persefoni.md)) `Enterprise` `Paid` `AI-Powered` `API`
- [Plan A](https://plana.earth/) - Corporate carbon management and ESG reporting platform focused on European CSRD compliance. Combines carbon accounting with decarbonization action plans and supplier engagement. Paid SaaS subscription. ([Read more](/details/plan-a.md)) `Paid` `CSRD` `Enterprise` `SME`
- [Salesforce Net Zero Cloud](https://www.salesforce.com/products/net-zero-cloud/overview) - Sustainability management cloud built on Salesforce for carbon accounting, ESG reporting, and climate action planning. Integrates with Salesforce CRM and supports scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions tracking. Paid enterprise subscription. ([Read more](/details/salesforce-net-zero-cloud.md)) `Enterprise` `Paid` `Salesforce` `CRM`
- [Sweep](https://sweep.net/) - Carbon accounting and ESG platform for enterprises to track emissions, engage suppliers, and meet CSRD and other regulatory requirements. Offers data collection, scenario modeling, and reporting workflows. Paid enterprise subscription. ([Read more](/details/sweep.md)) `Enterprise` `Paid` `Scope 3` `CSRD`
- [Watershed](https://watershed.com/) - Enterprise carbon accounting platform that helps large companies measure, report, and reduce emissions across scopes 1, 2, and 3. It aggregates utility, spend, and activity data for climate disclosure and decarbonization planning. Paid enterprise subscription. ([Read more](/details/watershed.md)) `Enterprise` `Paid` `API` `Scope 3`

## Carbon APIs & Emission Factors

- [Climatiq](https://climatiq.io/) - Carbon emissions API providing access to a database of emission factors and lifecycle assessment data. Enables developers to estimate carbon footprints from activity data. Paid API with free tier. ([Read more](/details/climatiq.md)) `API` `Paid` `Emission Factors` `Developer`
- [Cloverly](https://cloverly.com/) - Carbon offset and emissions API that calculates the carbon impact of transactions, travel, and shipping in real time. Provides access to offset projects and emission factors. Paid API with usage-based pricing. ([Read more](/details/cloverly.md)) `API` `Paid` `Offsets` `Developer`
- [Lune](https://lune.co/) - Carbon emissions API for businesses to calculate and offset the carbon footprint of transactions, flights, and shipping. Provides developer tools and emission factor data. Paid API. ([Read more](/details/lune.md)) `API` `Paid` `Emission Factors` `Developer`
- [Patch](https://patch.io/) - Carbon removal and climate API platform for integrating carbon offsetting and emissions estimation into digital products. Offers API access to carbon credits and climate data. Paid API. ([Read more](/details/patch.md)) `API` `Paid` `Carbon Removal` `Developer`

- [Sphera](https://sphera.com/) - Enterprise ESG, risk, and product stewardship software suite including emissions management, EHS, and supply chain risk. Serves industrial and manufacturing organizations with complex compliance needs. Paid enterprise license. ([Read more](/details/sphera.md)) `Enterprise` `Paid` `Supply Chain` `EHS`

## Open Source

- [Cloud Carbon Footprint](https://www.cloudcarbonfootprint.org/) - Open-source tool to estimate and measure carbon emissions from cloud usage across AWS, Azure, and GCP. Provides CLI and web interface for cloud carbon monitoring. Apache 2.0 licensed. ([Read more](/details/cloud-carbon-footprint.md)) `Open Source` `Free` `Cloud` `API` `Self-Hosted`
- [CodeCarbon](https://codecarbon.io/) - Open-source Python package that estimates CO2 emissions from code execution and computing workloads. Tracks energy consumption and carbon impact of software. Free to use. ([Read more](/details/codecarbon.md)) `Open Source` `Free` `Python` `Developer` `API`

- [OpenLCA](https://www.openlca.org/) - Open-source life cycle assessment and carbon footprint software for product and system modeling. Supports ISO 14040/44 and connects to LCA databases. Free to download and modify. ([Read more](/details/openlca.md)) `Open Source` `Free` `LCA` `Desktop`
